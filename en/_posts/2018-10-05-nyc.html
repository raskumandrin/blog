Plan

New York

Departure at two in the afternoon, eight hours of flying, arrival at three in the afternoon. The body has a hard time orienting itself in a day like that. The suitcase with clothes, a jacket, fifteen pairs of socks, a MacBook, chargers, a DNA test, and the long-suffering poi somehow did not arrive. Instead they gave me a Mastercard with 70 euros. So now I am across the ocean traveling light, with a little backpack containing a work laptop, a few documents, and a hoodie. Unusual.

Instagram

Day two. Both phones - a Nokia dialer and an iPhone 4s - are pumpkins here. Neither the home SIM card nor local ones work in them. The frequencies, apparently, are wrong. It seems the iPhone can still be revived through some unusual operators. In the offices of the major operators they just shrug.

They say the suitcase has been found. And supposedly it is flying. But there are no other details. Flying where? From where?

Instagram

Manhattan is stunningly beautiful from the outside and from the inside. Especially on a clear evening. From inside, it is impossible to photograph anything - the skyscrapers barely fit into three or four consecutive frames, and there is nothing on the phone to stitch them together.

Finnair said that the suitcase did make it to New York and is currently living somewhere there; they advised waiting for a call from the delivery service.
