I was mostly concerned about whether it would be possible to transfer my progress of 58 days, 2 hours, and 18 minutes (🤦‍♂) from Xbox to Steam. I did some googling beforehand but couldn't find a straightforward guide saying, "do this, then that, and it'll all work out."

But it worked out!

Here's a screenshot from Steam after the transfer. All achievements also transferred.

Before:

After, with the Ford ♡ :

The procedure looked like this:

  1. Purchased SnowRunner on both platforms, including the first, second, and third years.
  2. Registered with PROS.
  3. From the in-game menu on both platforms, linked to PROS via QR code.
  4. Selected "send save to PROS" on Xbox.
  5. On the PROS website, selected "Save Transfer" — three screenshots in the post header.
  6. Launched the game on Steam:
    — Looks like your save has been pulled in here. Apply?
    — Apply
    — Done
