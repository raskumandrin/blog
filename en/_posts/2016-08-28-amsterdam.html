Flipping through the magazine on the plane, I saw a picture.

I decided to visit that tower (and did not regret it later).

KLM offers beer - very tasty. Offers it if you ask, that is; it is hidden on the cart otherwise.

From the airport, there is a train to the central station for about 5 euros.

There is a piano at the station - you can play it.

From the other side of the station, the tower is visible from the embankment. The free ferry leaves from there as well.

The elevator ceiling is glass, and this is what happens there.

At the top there is a bar and an observation deck. Off to the side there is also a model of Amsterdam. A projector is mounted above the gray little buildings.

The view of Amsterdam from the observation deck.
