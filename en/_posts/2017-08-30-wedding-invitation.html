Five years ago we had a wedding. Of course, we needed to make invitations for the guests. We decided not to make paper ones, but electronic ones.

We sent everyone logins and passwords. The texts were different for different guests. From the inside, we checked that everyone logged in and read theirs.

This is how it worked. Any login and password will work. "Sonny" in the example is Nadya's brother.
