I ended up in Mae Hong Son - a small town in northern Thailand - by accident. On the way to Wat Tam Wua monastery, the minibus driver forgot to drop me off and took me all the way to the final stop.

The only difference from other small towns in northern Thailand that caught my eye was the beautiful tiered roofs.

The only decent map of the town I came across.

After spending the night in some guesthouse for 100 rubles, I left early in the morning for Wat Tam Wua monastery.
