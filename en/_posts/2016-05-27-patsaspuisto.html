Veijo Ronkkonen was a Finnish folk artist and sculptor who created a park of unusual sculptures in the garden of his home. Patsaspuisto means sculpture park in Finnish; it is located in the municipality of Parikkala, in the village of Koitsanlahti, near the Russian border.

We ended up there on a rainy evening at sunset. In the twilight, these frozen figures with glass eyes and real teeth looked fairly eerie.
