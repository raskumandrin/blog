At first I wanted to make an image of the whole wall as one very, very wide picture. But Photoshop rejected the attempt - supposedly it did not have enough memory to stitch together some one hundred and fifty pictures. And in general, as it turned out, stitching is quite a task: the edges do not match, the sky is a different color everywhere, and so on.

In the end I chose several series and, without worrying too much about quality, joined the images together somehow. Most of all I like the portrait of the girl (and not only me, as people say).

This year the festival will take place in Saint Petersburg from July 25 to 27. I will definitely go and see it.

Meeting Of Styles

Because of its authentical style and its message the Meeting is mentioned as the best Graffiti-Event ever happened. Its worldwide reputation and the enourmous feedback inspired us in 2002 to spread its message all over the world which led to the founding of the “Int. Meeting Of Styles”. Since its foundation more than 70 events were realised and attracted more than 100.000 spectators throughout the world.
