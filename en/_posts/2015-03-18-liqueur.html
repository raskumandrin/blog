The only processed chokeberry product I like is liqueur. Grandma's recipe, as usual.

For half a liter of cold water:

  • 100 cherry leaves;
  • 1-1.5 cups of chokeberries.

Boil for 10 minutes.

Cool.

Squeeze.

Mix into the remaining drink until dissolved:

  • 400 g granulated sugar;
  • 0.5 liters of vodka.

Bottle it and let it stand from autumn at least until New Year. But if it stands for a year or more, it only gets tastier.
