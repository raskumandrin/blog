--- url: grandma-muffins-recipe title: Grandma's Muffins. Recipe categories: things tags: [recipes] ---

Beat two eggs with one cup of sugar.

Melt 100-200 grams of margarine and pour it into the beaten eggs.

200 grams of sweet curd mass with raisins. Salt to taste.

Mix one and a half to two cups of flour with half a spoon of baking soda, sift. Add a spoon of lemon juice instead of quenching with vinegar.

Put into molds and bake at 180 degrees.
